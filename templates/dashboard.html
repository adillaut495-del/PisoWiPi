Reading a row: Device is the MAC the hotspot knows, with the IP and how long ago the console last heard from it. Paid for lists the credits that built the session, newest last. Time left counts the real clock down to the end time, and the bar shows how much of the granted time is still unspent. Idle means the device has been quiet for {{ idle_seconds // 60 }} minutes or more while its clock runs. Pause banks the remaining minutes so none are lost, Resume puts them back on the clock, and Kick ends the session and drops the device off a live hotspot.
